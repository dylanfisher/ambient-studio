Track

A track plays sketches of one brief one after another, each for a part of its own timeline, with a crossfade or a morph from one to the next. Add sketches, then choose a section to move it, set where it starts in its sketch and how long it plays, and set its crossfade or morph. Drag a block's edges, use the fields, or use the keys shown on each button. Play a section to hear its part of the sketch. Save keeps a new version and leaves the earlier ones as they were. Render a saved version to play the whole track, hear one crossfade on its own, and export it as WAV and FLAC, with a stem for each voice if you want them. Under the timeline, a region attached to the brief can be drawn stretched to the track's length, beside each section you have played; Material places the best candidates of the brief's map and steers a section along one of its directions.

    Macro lanes

    A lane moves one macro across the whole track, on top of what each section's sketch does with it: each point adds to or takes from the sketch's own value, from −0.5 to 0.5, and 0 changes nothing. Choose a lane, then add points and move them by the fields, the buttons and their keys, or by dragging a point across in time or up and down.
