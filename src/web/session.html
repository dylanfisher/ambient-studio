Session

Press Ask for new candidates and wait: the model writes each candidate and the studio renders a 25-second excerpt of it, which appears below as soon as it is ready. Each row is one candidate, and one made from another sits indented under it. Play each one, then rate it with the buttons on its row; Hide moves it to Hidden candidates at the bottom, where it can be shown again. The marks beside a row count how often it was chosen (▲) and not chosen (▼) in A/B comparisons. Under each row are its Less / More choices, note and sketch, explore and nudge; Show the numbers adds its locks, measurements and the other numbers. Thumbs and the Less / More choices can be changed or cleared at any time. The lineage page shows where every candidate of the brief came from.
