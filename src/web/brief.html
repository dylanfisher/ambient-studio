Brief

A brief tells the model what to make. Write a name and a description, set any rules every candidate must follow, and save. Then press Start a session: the studio asks the model for candidates and renders an excerpt of each, and you listen and rate them on the session page. Your ratings and sketches are listed further down.

The brief

Rules every candidate must follow (leave a field empty to leave it free)

Ranges to aim for (leave both ends empty for no range)

The studio measures each excerpt. The model is told these ranges and aims inside them, but a candidate outside one is still kept.

Each range runs from the region's 10th to its 90th percentile, at least a set width. The fields show what is saved now beside them. Nothing is saved until you press Save.

              slider to steer from it. Leave the first end empty to start from the sound of this
