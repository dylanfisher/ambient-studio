Brief

A brief tells the model what to make. Write a name and a description, set any rules every candidate must follow, and save. Then press Start a session: the studio asks the model for candidates and renders an excerpt of each, and you listen and rate them on the session page. Your ratings and sketches are listed further down.

The brief

Describe the brief

Say in your own words how the brief should be, such as slow, dark, underwater drone, almost no rhythm, a little bell now and then. The model fills in the name, description, rules and ranges below. Nothing is saved until you press Save.

Rules every candidate must follow (leave a field empty to leave it free)

Ranges to aim for (leave both ends empty for no range)

The studio measures each excerpt. The model is told these ranges and aims inside them, but a candidate outside one is still kept.

Each range runs from the region's 10th to its 90th percentile, at least a set width. The fields show what is saved now beside them. Nothing is saved until you press Save.

        Regions for the model to aim at
        
            
        
              Attach a region you marked on the References page. Every request for candidates
              tells the model the region's measurements and a few words for its sound, and asks
              it to aim at them, not to copy the recording.
            
        
            
          
            
          
            
          
              
              
            
          
          
          

          
          
            
          Directions for the sliders
          
            
          
              Type two ends, such as dry and underwater. Every candidate on a session page then
              shows where it sits between this brief's least and most of that direction, with a
              slider to steer from it. Leave the first end empty to start from this brief's
              description.
            
          
            
            
            
            
            
            
              
              
              
            
            
          
            

          
            
            
            Breed two sketches
            
            
            
              Choose two sketches you kept. The model writes a few children that take parts of
              both, and each child is then nudged once. The children appear on your newest open
              session, or on a new one when every session is stopped.
            
            
            
            
              
              
              
            
            
            
            
          
            

          
            
            
            Tracks
            
            
            
              A track plays sketches you kept, one after another, each for a part of its own
              timeline, with a crossfade between them. Make one here, then open it to arrange it.
            
            
            
              
            
              
              
              
            
              
            
              
              Or have the model propose one: give the length and the arc in words, such as
              emerge, plateau, dissolve. Its proposal opens on the timeline as the track's first
              version, to reorder, resize and retime before you render it.
            
              
            
              
              
              
              
              
            
              
            
              
            
              
          
              

          
              
            
              Axes for the map
              
            
              
              Choose two or three things to place this brief's candidates by, such as dark to
              bright by static to evolving. The map page then shows the best candidate in each
              cell and fills the empty ones. A direction or a region chosen here needs its sound
              analysis. Choosing again replaces the axes; no new version of the brief is saved.
