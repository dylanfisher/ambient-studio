Quick A/B

This session's candidates, two at a time: listen, then choose the better one, and the next pair comes at once. The one you choose stays to meet the next candidate, until every candidate has had a turn; choose Random pairs to hear pairs in any order instead, or Best questions to hear first the pairs your taste model is least sure how to order, which teaches it the most. No order plays the same pair twice. Best questions needs the session ranked by a taste model fitted on the Model test page. The keys are on the buttons: ← and → play the left and right excerpt from the same point, A or D chooses left or right, S says they are about the same, W skips, Space pauses or plays, and Z undoes the last choice. Keys do nothing while you are typing in a field.

Searching by ear: the one that stays is the best so far, and the other is a new variation near it, chosen to learn the most about what you like. If you choose the new one, it becomes the best and stays. The next variation is rendered while you listen. Each choice is saved as an ordinary A/B choice, so it also teaches your taste model. About the same and Skip move on too; Skip tells the search nothing. Undo is off here, since the search has already used your choice. Stop ends the search; its best is shown first on the session page.

Spot check for the optimizer test: each pair is one excerpt from a search and one from its control, a nudge of the same start, and which side is which was drawn at random and isn't shown. Choose the one you like better, by ear alone. Each choice is saved as an ordinary A/B choice, so it also teaches your taste model, and the Model test page counts them. About the same and Skip aren't counted as a choice either way. Undo is off here.
